# World income and population in 2024 and 2025

An offline snapshot of World Development Indicators for 217 economies.
Regional and global aggregates are excluded. Missing estimates are NA.

## Usage

``` r
world_income
```

## Format

A data frame with 434 rows and six columns:

- iso3, country, region:

  World Bank three-letter economy code, name and region.

- year:

  Calendar year.

- gni_per_capita:

  Gross national income per capita, Atlas method, in current US dollars.

- population:

  Total population in people.

## Source

World Bank,
[income](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GNP.PCAP.CD) and
[population](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL), November
2026 snapshot.
